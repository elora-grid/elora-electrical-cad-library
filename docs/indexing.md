# Search discovery status — 18 November 2026

The live [equipment sitemap index](https://eloragrid.ai/cad-sitemaps/index.xml) covers all 76,512 published CAD equipment pages. Its two leaves contain 45,000 and 31,512 URLs. The [master sitemap](https://eloragrid.ai/sitemap-index.xml) also links the general site sitemap and both CAD image sitemaps, covering 49,841 distinct equipment pages with previews. Image entries overlap the equipment catalogue; do not add them to the equipment count.

The refreshed master, CAD and image sitemaps were submitted through Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. IndexNow accepted all 76,512 CAD URLs in eight batches, each returning HTTP 200. [Public submission receipt](indexing-20261118.json).

The live audit verified exact equipment sitemap coverage, current metadata fingerprints, canonical catalogue metadata, Dataset structured data, LLM navigation and crawl rules. Existing sitemap URLs remain available. The website's daily IndexNow workflow continues to notify meaningful public-content changes.

These results establish successful submission and technical discovery checks. They do not establish that Google or Bing has indexed every page or promise indexing times or rankings. Search Console and Bing reports can retain older discovery counts while refreshed leaves are processed.
