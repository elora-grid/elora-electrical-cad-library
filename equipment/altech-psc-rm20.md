## What the equipment does

A power-supply redundancy module combines suitable supply paths so the connected load can remain powered if one source is lost, within the designed arrangement.

In a substation control panel, it can connect two independent DC supplies to a common supported load.

Use an outline or mounting drawing to plan where the equipment fits and where it attaches. Use a connection drawing to trace its electrical interfaces. A 3D model helps coordinate space; check the manufacturer's installation requirements separately.
