## What the DVC 350 does

DEIF DVC 350 digital automatic voltage regulator: manufacturer STEP geometry and a dimensioned datasheet view for generator-cabinet layouts.

In a generator control assembly, this regulator controls alternator excitation. The drawing helps place its board and fixings while the electrical design uses the matching installation documentation.

Use dvc-350-3d-step-file.stp for the complete supplied regulator geometry in a 3D layout. Use dvc-350-data-sheet-4921240615-uk.pdf, page 7, for the dimensioned plan, fixing pattern and overall-height table. The dvc-350-3d-pdf.pdf file contains interactive PDF content. Use a compatible PDF application; it is not a flat dimension drawing.

- Use the published 52.9 mm overall height when reserving depth. The 19 mm callout on the side illustration is a different local dimension.
- The fixing centres shown are 115 × 100 mm. Keep the 140 × 125 mm outer footprint separate when setting out the mounting plate.
- The layout files describe physical space. Confirm alternator excitation and connection details in the DVC 350 installation documentation.

### Is the regulator only 19 mm high?

No. The side illustration includes a 19 mm local callout, but the dimensions table gives a 52.9 mm overall height. Use the overall-height value when reserving cabinet depth.
