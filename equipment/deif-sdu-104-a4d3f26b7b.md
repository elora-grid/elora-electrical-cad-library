## What the SDU 104 does

DEIF SDU 104 marine engine shutdown unit: editable DWG outlines, dimensioned PDF views and a STEP housing model for cabinet layouts. The datasheet supplies mounting and size references.

An engine-control cabinet can use this unit as an additional shutdown layer. Its drawing helps position the unit and its terminal sides on the mounting rail.

Choose sdu-104-2d-cad-drawing.dwg for editable housing views in your cabinet GA. Choose sdu-104-2d-pdf.pdf for the manufacturer outline: the marked spans are 156.0, 125.4 and 59.1. The matching datasheet expresses the nominal housing dimensions in millimetres. Choose sdu-104-3d-step-file.stp for the supplied housing and terminal geometry in a 3D cabinet layout. Use sdu-104-data-sheet-4921240625-uk.pdf, page 16, for nominal dimensions, DIN mounting and weight. The optional sdu-104-3d-pdf.pdf uses interactive PDF content. Choose STEP for the website model viewer, or use a compatible PDF application for that original.

- Keep the detailed outline dimensions separate from the rounded datasheet values: the outline marks 125.4 and 59.1, while the nominal table states 125 mm and 59 mm.
- This is a marine engine shutdown unit. Confirm the selected engine-controller arrangement against the SDU 104 documentation before specifying it.
- Locate the terminal sides shown on the drawing so cabinet wiring can reach them. The outline is not a terminal-function or field-wiring diagram.

### Why do the outline and datasheet dimensions differ slightly?

The one-sheet outline marks 156.0, 125.4 and 59.1. Data sheet 4921240625G lists rounded nominal dimensions of 156 × 125 × 59 mm. Keep the detailed drawing with your layout instead of replacing its annotations with rounded table values.
