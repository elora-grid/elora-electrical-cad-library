## What the equipment does

A station-post insulator supports an energised conductor while separating it electrically from its grounded support. Its end fittings, height and mechanical strength determine how it can be mounted.

When positioning 900 EU on a substation support, use PDF page 10 to compare its mounting interfaces with the structure and conductor connection. The listed Height (in) is 80.
