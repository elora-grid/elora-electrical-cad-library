## What the equipment does

A residual-current device compares the current leaving a circuit with the current returning. A difference can indicate current flowing along an unintended path, causing the device to trip. An RCBO also provides overcurrent protection.

In a substation services board, it can protect a suitable maintenance-outlet circuit by detecting a current imbalance.
