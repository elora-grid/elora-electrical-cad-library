## What this resource helps you do

This manufacturer resource helps you find drawings or select versions of switch-disconnectors, transfer switches, auxiliary contacts. Use it to locate the matching equipment outline or model for your design.

When planning a microgrid installation, you can use this collection to find the selected switch-disconnectors drawing before reserving its space, mounting points and cable routes in the layout.

Use an outline or mounting drawing to plan where the equipment fits and where it attaches. Use a connection drawing to trace its electrical interfaces. A 3D model helps coordinate space; check the manufacturer's installation requirements separately.
